## Verify a particular segment

1. Record the road reference, endpoints, OSM way ID, travel direction, date and vehicle category. For STOP or Give way, check the approach's stop line. A sign visible from the main road may apply only to a side street.
2. Compare a current road-authority document with dated imagery or a field observation when available. Check the start and end of the limit, supplementary plates, geographic scope, temporary signage and the settlement signs. Boundaries and built-up areas in maps are approximations.
3. Resolve disagreements by checking the dates, locations, directions and conditions of each source. A permanent offline correction should be limited to the documented geometry and retain the source and date.
4. Replay parallel roads, ramps, the opposite direction, the point after passing the sign, and stale position data. Use urban and rural samples with explicit, conditional and missing limits. Report coverage, exact matches, false alerts on the wrong road or direction, and the number of unverified segments separately.

## Offline policy and current pack

- A numeric directional OSM limit takes precedence over an inferred default. A grey ring identifies the inferred display. Opposite directions with different values require the direction to be resolved. OSM `trunk` can include European roads. The pack identifies expressways for its defaults by the DEx/DX reference. [Romanian OSM classification](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Ro:Map_Features) · [Official DX 12 example](https://mfinante.gov.ro/static/10/Mfp/transparenta/proiectbuget2025/Ministerul_Transporturilor_Infrastructurii.pdf)
- Variable or conditional values are suppressed for the relevant direction by the offline matcher. Physical signs take precedence, and the pack may lag temporary changes. [Conditional tags](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Conditional_restrictions) · [Variable limits](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Key:maxspeed:variable)
- Priority alerts need fresh position data, consecutive matches and an unambiguous approach in the driven direction. Signs on a roundabout ring or with unresolved applicability are omitted. A dated authority notice or field evidence is needed to confirm the actual sign.
- The pack has 988,033 road pieces and 19,739 matched priority records. The local limits file records the verified Calea Victoriei segments and two Strada Rondă segments in Sector 2. [Local corrections](kit/tools/romania-local-limits.json) · [Sector 2 notice](https://adp2.ro/wp-content/uploads/Informare-sensuri-unice-cvartal-4.pdf)

Build the pack with `kit/tools/pack_sli.py --local-limits kit/tools/romania-local-limits.json`. Use the same extract with `kit/tools/pack_junctions.py --speed-pack <compiled-pack>` to produce the junction data. Automated checks are in `kit/tools/test_pack_sli.py`, `kit/tools/test_speed_limit_ro.js`, `kit/tools/test_speed_limit_outputs.js` and `kit/tools/test_speed_limit_power.js`. Replay tests measure internal consistency. Accuracy against current road signs requires a separate field sample.
